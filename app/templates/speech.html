Academic Overview: Speech Processing Domain

Speech Processing in NLP deals with analyzing human speech signals and converting spoken language into structured textual representations using acoustic recognition algorithms. In investigation intelligence systems like TRACE-X, speech processing enables automatic transcription of audio recordings (e.g. emergency calls, suspect interviews, intercepted voice messages), preparing raw audio evidence for downstream text classification, entity extraction, and evidence correlation.
